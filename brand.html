The mark is a keyboard and a mixer at once.

Six bars read as piano keys, as faders and as MIDI blocks. The three bars with stems are the "black keys". In v2 each key takes one colour from the pop palette, left to right: orange, pink, lime, violet, sky, sunshine.

Colour palette

A warm base, turned up.

v1 was charcoal, cream, burnt orange, olive and warm grey: grown-up, but quiet for a teen audience. v2 keeps charcoal and cream as the base, pushes the orange brighter, and adds five pop colours. Olive and warm grey stay on as supporting neutrals for print and adult-facing pieces.

Typography

Bold headlines. Easy reading.

Aa

Montserrat

ExtraBold 800 and Bold 700. Headlines, buttons, labels and the wordmark. Tight tracking (-0.03 to -0.045em) on big sizes.

Aa

Inter

Regular 400, Medium 500 and SemiBold 600. Body copy, UI and long reading. Normal tracking.

Tagline and voice

Learn. Create. Produce.

Primary tagline first, then supporting lines for headlines and social:

  • Learn. Create. Produce.
  • Hear it. Play it.
  • Real skills. Real music. Real progress.
  • Music skills for a bigger you.
  • From ideas to sound.
  • Your track. Live. Loud.

We sound like the coolest teacher you ever had: encouraging, direct, a little playful, never cheesy.

  • Talk to the learner. "You'll play your first song" beats "Students will acquire skills".
  • Promise outcomes, not hours. A song you can play. A beat you made.
  • Respect beginners. No jargon without a quick explanation. Nobody is "too old" or "behind".
  • Speak to teens and parents at once. Fun up front, reassurance close behind.

Real rooms. Real smiles. A splash of colour.

Candid, warmly lit, mid-moment: someone just nailed the chord, or the beat just dropped. Real gear in view (keyboards, pads, laptops running a DAW), with colour coming from LED light, acoustic panels and clothing rather than filters. Show teens and adults, and a mix of ages, backgrounds and genders.

Usage

Keep it loud, keep it clean.

Do

  • Use the full-colour mark on charcoal or cream
  • Lead with one pop colour per section
  • Use the one-colour mark on photos and busy backgrounds
  • Pair big Montserrat headlines with short Inter body copy

Don't

  • Recolour individual bars or change their order
  • Stretch, rotate or add effects to the logo
  • Put the full-colour mark on orange, pink or lime
  • Use all six pop colours in one layout (the mark aside)
