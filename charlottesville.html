Ways to learn in the studio

One to one, or with your crew.

Private lessons

Forty-five minutes one to one each week, for teens and adults. Piano by ear, production, or a mix of both. Online Hub included.

Teen Producer Lab

Ages 12 to 18. A weekly 90-minute small group of no more than six: beats, keys, songwriting and a premiere at Showcase Night.

Adult evening classes

Six-week group courses after work: Piano by Ear for Beginners, Worship Keys, and Make a Beat in Fender Studio.

Summer Beat Camp

A week-long day camp for ages 11 to 16. Every camper leaves with a finished, mixed track and plays it at Friday's listening party.

Ages 12 to 18

The Teen Producer Lab.

An extracurricular crew for teens who live in their headphones, after school or at the weekend. Each week the crew learns a new production or keys skill, works on their own track, and plays it for the group. Every season ends on stage at Showcase Night.

  • Small groups of no more than six
  • Weekday evenings from 5 PM, or Saturdays and Sundays
  • Laptops, MIDI keyboards, pads and headphones provided
  • Fender Studio, plus the free version to keep making music at home
  • Online Hub and the Saturday Teen Beat Club call-in included
  • A monthly progress note for parents
