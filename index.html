How it works

From first lesson to first track.

No audition and no experience needed. Your teacher meets you where you are and builds the plan around the music you actually listen to.

1

Try a free lesson

Thirty minutes in the studio or on video. Play us something, or nothing at all. We'll find your starting point.

2

Get your path

Piano by ear, production, or both. You get a level, a weekly practice plan and songs picked for you.

3

Learn, call in, show off

Lessons every week, a live call-in when you're stuck, and a stage at Showcase Night when you're ready.

Why it works

Real skills. Real music. Real progress.

We teach the way working musicians actually learn: by listening, copying, experimenting and playing with other people.

Ear first

Learn chords and progressions as sounds you recognise, so you can pick out songs from the radio, from church or from your playlist.

Real software

Students produce in Fender Studio, a full professional DAW that also has a free version, so practice at home looks just like lessons.

Live every week

Group call-ins on the Online Hub keep you moving. Bring a question, a song or a half-finished beat.

Feedback on your work

Upload a playing video or a track and get notes from a teacher: what's working, and the one thing to fix next.

A crew to grow with

Small groups, a friendly community and monthly beat challenges. Musicians get better faster together.

Something to show for it

Every season ends with Showcase Night: students play live and premiere what they made for family and friends.
